# BRAND GUIDELINES

## Vision

This portfolio represents who I am as a UX/UI Designer.

The experience should feel like stepping inside the workspace of a designer who enjoys researching problems, exploring ideas, creating thoughtful user experiences, and refining every detail.

The website should communicate curiosity, professionalism, creativity, and attention to detail without relying on excessive animations or unnecessary visual effects.

Every page should feel connected and part of one complete experience.

---

## Brand Statement

I design intuitive digital experiences by combining research, creativity, and thoughtful design decisions.

I believe good design isn't just about making interfaces beautiful—it is about solving real problems for real people.

---

## Personality

The portfolio should feel:

- Professional
- Modern
- Creative
- Friendly
- Thoughtful
- Human-centred
- Research-driven
- Detail-oriented
- Premium
- Minimal

---

## The Portfolio Should Never Feel

- Generic
- Template-like
- Corporate
- Overly playful
- Cluttered
- Flashy
- Over-animated
- Slow
- Confusing

---

## Core Design Values

Every design decision should support these values.

- Research before design.
- Simplicity is powerful.
- Accessibility is never optional.
- Every interaction should have a purpose.
- Consistency builds trust.
- Small details create memorable experiences.
- Users should always feel in control.

---

## Storytelling Theme

The entire portfolio is inspired by the UX design process.

Instead of feeling like separate pages, the website should feel like one continuous journey through how I approach design.

Each page represents a different stage of the UX process while sharing one consistent visual language.

The experience should feel cohesive from beginning to end.

---

## Emotional Goal

When recruiters finish exploring the website they should think:

"This designer is thoughtful."

"This designer understands UX."

"This designer pays attention to details."

"I would like to interview this designer."

---

## Motion Philosophy

Animations should support usability.

Motion should:

- Guide attention
- Explain hierarchy
- Reinforce interactions
- Provide feedback
- Feel smooth and natural

Motion should never exist purely for decoration.

Subtle animations are preferred over dramatic effects.

---

## Cursor Philosophy

The custom cursor represents curiosity and exploration.

It should feel elegant and lightweight.

It should enhance interactions without reducing usability or making the website difficult to use.

Users should never feel like the cursor is slowing them down.

---

## Consistency

Every page must feel like part of the same product.

Typography, spacing, colours, components, animations, and interactions must all follow one shared design language.

Nothing should feel copied from another website or disconnected from the rest of the experience.
